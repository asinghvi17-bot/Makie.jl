## Changed

- Converting meshes for `mesh`, `poly` (and so `barplot`, `hist`, `pie`) now gives meshes of concrete types, so these conversions are inferable for ahead-of-time compilation (`juliac --trim`, `WasmCodegen`). This holds for meshes (and vectors of them) whose vertex attributes are plain `Vector`s, such as the meshes `poly` makes of `Rect`s and `Polygon`s. Meshes with `FaceView`s (per-face normals, uvs or colors, as `normal_mesh` makes them), attributes that are not `Vector`s (views, ranges) and the conversion of geometry primitives (`Rect3f`, ...) still convert, but through the parts of GeometryBasics that are not inferable (`expand_faceviews`, the `Mesh` constructor, `convert_facetype`), so they are not compiled ahead of time yet.
- An empty vector of meshes of a concrete type converts to an empty mesh of the type that non-empty vectors of it convert to (was `Mesh{N, Float32}`). Vectors of meshes are merged into meshes with `Vector`s for attributes and faces, also if the meshes have views or ranges for them, and an empty vector of such meshes now converts to an empty mesh instead of throwing a `MethodError`.
- The dim conversion of an empty `Vector{<:VecTypes}` is empty with the type of the components of its points if that is a concrete number type, and `Float64` otherwise (as it was; it is never `Any`, which a `Tuple{Date, Float64}` has as the join of its components).
