## Changed

- `poly` (and so `barplot`, `hist`, `pie`) triangulates polygons with `Makie.earcut_triangulate`, a pure Julia port of mapbox's earcut (earcut.hpp 2.2.4), instead of calling `libearcut` through `GeometryBasics.earcut_triangulate`, so that these plots compile ahead of time (`juliac --trim`, Wasm). The triangles are identical to `libearcut`'s, in the same order, on the polygons it was tested with (random, degenerate and recipe-shaped polygons, with holes); the exception is the order in which holes with the same leftmost x are linked into the outline when there are more than 16 of them, where `std::sort` in `libearcut` and the stable sort of the port may order equal holes differently, giving a different (but valid) triangulation. It is also faster than the `ccall` as made from Julia (a rectangle: about 50 ns against 1.5 µs).
